Project Creation Guidelines

Title Guidelines

Use descriptive, professional titles that clearly identify your project. Examples: "Residential Complex Design - Phase 1", "USPTO Patent Illustration for Medical Device".

Description Best Practices

Provide comprehensive project details including objectives, scope, deliverables, and outcomes. Include technical specifications, challenges overcome, and innovative solutions implemented.

Category Selection

Select all relevant categories to improve project discoverability. Multiple categories help visitors find your work through different search paths.

Technology Documentation

List all software, tools, and technologies used. Common examples: AutoCAD, MS Visio, Civil 3D, ArcGIS, AERMOD, MATLAB, Python, etc.

Image Requirements

Upload high-quality images that showcase your work. Recommended formats: JPG, PNG. Optimal dimensions: 1200x800px or higher for thumbnails.

Featured Projects

Mark your best work as featured to highlight it on the homepage. Featured projects should represent your top-quality work and diverse skills.

Client Information

Include client names only if you have permission. For confidential projects, use generic terms like "Private Client" or "Government Agency".
