Inquiry Management Tips

Response Best Practices

  • • Respond to inquiries within 24 hours
  • • Use the pre-filled email templates for quick replies
  • • Always acknowledge receipt of inquiry first
  • • Ask clarifying questions if requirements are unclear

Status Management

  • • Keep status updated for better tracking
  • • Use "In Progress" when you start working
  • • Mark "Completed" only after delivery
  • • Document cancellation reasons for future reference

Client Communication

  • • Maintain professional tone in all communications
  • • Provide regular updates on project progress
  • • Be clear about timelines and deliverables
  • • Follow up after project completion

Opportunity Tracking

  • • Review inquiry patterns to identify trends
  • • Analyze which services get most inquiries
  • • Use data to improve service offerings
  • • Follow up on incomplete inquiries
